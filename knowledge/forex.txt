Forex is the foreign exchange market where currencies are traded.

A currency pair contains a base currency and a quote currency.

EUR/USD means the euro is the base currency and the US dollar is the quote currency.

A pip is a commonly used unit for measuring price movement in forex.

Spread is the difference between the bid price and ask price.

Leverage increases both potential gains and potential losses.

Risk management is important when learning forex.

London and New York are major forex trading sessions.

CPI, NFP and FOMC events can cause significant market volatility.

Technical analysis studies price and market data.

Fundamental analysis studies economic and financial factors.
