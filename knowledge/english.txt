English is a language used for communication around the world.

Hello means a greeting.

Crazy means behaving in a strange, unusual, or unreasonable way.

Good means satisfactory, positive, or of a high quality.

Improve means to make something better.

Learn means to gain knowledge or a skill.

Example sentence:
I am learning Python.

Present simple is commonly used for habits and general facts.

Past simple is commonly used for completed actions in the past.

Future forms can be used to talk about future plans and events.
